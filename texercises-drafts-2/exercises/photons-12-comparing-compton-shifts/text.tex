Photons are scattered by free electrons at rest. Their wavelength grows by $\Delta\lambda = \lambda_C\left(1-\cos\theta\right)$, where $\theta$ is the scattering angle and $\lambda_C = \frac{h}{m_e c}$ the Compton wavelength.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Calculate $\Delta\lambda$ for the scattering angles \angaO, \angbO, \angcO\ and \angdO. At which angle is the change of wavelength largest?

\abc Cu-$K_\alpha$ X-rays ($\lambda_X = \lxO$) and gamma rays ($\lambda_\gamma = \lgmO$) are both scattered by \angcO. Compare the changes of their wavelengths.

\abc Which photons lose the larger fraction of their energy, the X-ray or the gamma photons?
\end{abcliste}
