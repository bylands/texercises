Light, radio waves and microwaves all travel at the speed of light. Measuring the time an echo takes gives a distance.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc A laser pulse is sent from an observatory to a reflector that the Apollo astronauts left on the Moon. The reflected light comes back after $t_1 = \tmO$. How far away is the Moon?

\abc An airport radar sends out a short pulse of microwaves. The echo from an aeroplane arrives $t_2 = \tpO$ later. How far away is the aeroplane?
\end{abcliste}
