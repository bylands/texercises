An electron is confined to a region of $\Delta x = \xeO$, about the size of an atom. A grain of dust of \mdO\ is located to within $\Delta x = \xdO$. Use Heisenberg's relation $\Delta x\cdot\Delta p \geq \frac{h}{4\pi}$.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Find the smallest possible uncertainty of the momentum of the electron.
\abc Find the smallest possible uncertainty of its velocity.
\abc Find the smallest possible uncertainty of the velocity of the grain of dust.
\end{abcliste}
