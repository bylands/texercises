\begin{abcliste}
\abc Below the shortest wavelength the intensity is zero. The curve starts at $\lambda_\text{min} = \result{\lmP{-12}{2}}$.

\abc The most energetic photons get the whole kinetic energy $eU$ of an electron: $e U = \frac{h c}{\lambda_\text{min}}$ (Duane--Hunt).
\begin{align}
 U &= \UxF \\
   &= \frac{\nch\times\ncc}{\nce\times\lm} \\
   &= \Ux \approx \result{\UxP{3}{2}}
\end{align}
The largest photon energy is $\ExP{3}{3}$.

\abc The lines belong to the anode atoms (jumps between their inner shells), not to the voltage. The peaks are at \SI{63}{\pico\m} and \SI{71}{\pico\m}: the anode is made of $\result{\text{molybdenum}}$.
\end{abcliste}
