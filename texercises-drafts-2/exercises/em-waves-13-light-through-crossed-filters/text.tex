Unpolarized light of intensity $I_0 = \IoO$ falls on two polarizing filters which are crossed: their axes are at \SI{90}{\degree} to each other.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc What is the intensity behind the first filter, and behind the second?

\abc A third filter is put between the two, its axis at $\theta = \alO$ to that of the first. What is now the intensity behind the middle filter, and behind the last?

\abc At what angle $\theta$ of the middle filter does the most light get through? What intensity is that?
\end{abcliste}
