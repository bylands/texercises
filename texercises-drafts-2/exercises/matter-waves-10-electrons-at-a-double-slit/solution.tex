\abc With $p = \sqrt{2 m_e e U}$ and $\lambda = h/p$:
\begin{align}
 \lambda &= \laF \\
   &= \frac{\nch}{\sqrt{2\times\ncme\times\nce\times\U}} \\
   &= \la \approx \result{\laP{-12}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc As for light, neighbouring bright fringes are $\Delta x = \lambda L/d$ apart (small angles):
\begin{align}
 \Delta x &= \dxF = \frac{\la\times\Ls}{\ds} = \dx \approx \result{\dxP{-6}{2}}
\end{align}
Tiny: that is why the pattern had to be magnified.

\abc $\lambda \propto 1/\sqrt{U}$: four times the voltage gives half the wavelength, so the fringe spacing \result{\text{halves}}.
