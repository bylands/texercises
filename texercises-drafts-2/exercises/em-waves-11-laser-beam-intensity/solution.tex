\begin{abcliste}
\abc The intensity is the power per area of the cross-section of the beam, a circle of diameter $d$:
\begin{align}
 I &= \IbF \\
   &= \frac{\Pl}{\pi\times\left(\db/2\right)^2} \\
   &= \Ib \approx \result{\IbP{3}{2}}
\end{align}
More than sunlight at noon, and the eye focuses it further onto a tiny spot of the retina: never look into a laser beam.

\abc From $I = \frac{1}{2}c\,\varepsilon_0\hat E^2$:
\begin{align}
 \hat E &= \EbF \\
   &= \sqrt{\frac{2\times\Ib}{\ncc\times\nceps}} \\
   &= \Eb \approx \result{\EbP{3}{2}}
\end{align}
\end{abcliste}
