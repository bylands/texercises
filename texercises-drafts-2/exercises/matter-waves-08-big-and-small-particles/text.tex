Three very different particles fly through a vacuum:
\begin{itemize}
 \item an electron at \velO,
 \item a C$_{60}$ molecule (a football of 60 carbon atoms, mass \mcuO) at \vcO,
 \item a grain of dust (\mdO) drifting at \vdO.
\end{itemize}

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Calculate the momentum of each particle.
\abc Calculate the de Broglie wavelength of each particle.
\abc The atoms of a crystal are about \SIrange{0.1}{0.3}{\nano\meter} apart. Which of the particles does a crystal diffract visibly?
\end{abcliste}
