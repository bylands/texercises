By what factor does the electric field strength change in each case?

\begin{abcliste}
\abc The charge of a point charge is doubled, and the point where the field is measured is moved to three times the distance.
\abc The charge of a long charged wire is tripled, and the point is moved to half the distance.
\abc The charge of a large charged plate is halved, and the point is moved to three times the distance.
\abc The charge of a plate capacitor is doubled, the area of its plates is halved, and the distance between them is tripled. (The field between the plates.)
\end{abcliste}
