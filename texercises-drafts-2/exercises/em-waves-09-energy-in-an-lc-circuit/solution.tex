\begin{abcliste}
\abc At the start, all the energy is in the electric field of the capacitor:
\begin{align}
 W &= \WoF \\
   &= \frac{1}{2}\times\Ca\times\left(\Uc\right)^2 \\
   &= \Wo \approx \result{\WoP{-3}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc A quarter of a period later the capacitor is empty and the current is largest: all the energy is now in the magnetic field of the coil.
\begin{align}
 \frac{1}{2}L\hat I^2 &= \frac{1}{2}C U^2 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \hat I = \IhF \\
   &= \Uc\times\sqrt{\frac{\Ca}{\Lc}} \\
   &= \Ih \approx \result{\IhP{0}{2}}
\end{align}
Then the energy flows back into the capacitor, which is charged the other way round, and so on.
\end{abcliste}
