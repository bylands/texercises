\begin{abcliste}
\abc The Sun radiates evenly in all directions: at the Earth, its power is spread over a sphere with radius $r_E$.
\begin{align}
 P &= \PsF \\
   &= 4\pi\times\left(\rE\right)^2\times\Sc \\
   &= \Ps \approx \result{\PsP{0}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc The intensity falls with $1/r^2$:
\begin{align}
 I &= \frac{S}{(r/r_E)^2} = \IMF \\
   &= \frac{\Sc}{\left(\kMO\right)^2} \\
   &= \IM \approx \result{\IMP{0}{2}}
\end{align}
The solar panels of a probe at Mars must be that much larger than near the Earth.
\end{abcliste}
