\begin{abcliste}
\abc Water molecules are neutral, but their charges are not evenly spread: they are dipoles. In the field of the rod they turn their negative end towards it. This end is closer to the rod, in the stronger field, so the attraction on it is larger than the repulsion on the far end: the molecules, and the jet, \emph{bend towards the rod}.

\abc The molecules now turn their positive end towards the rod, and the same argument holds: the jet bends \emph{towards the rod as well}, whatever the sign of its charge.
\end{abcliste}
