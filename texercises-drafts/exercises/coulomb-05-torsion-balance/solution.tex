\begin{abcliste}
\abc With $q$ the charge of each sphere, $r$ the distance and $F$ the force, Coulomb's law reads $F = k\,q^2/r^2$, so
\begin{align}
 q &= \qF \\
   &= \rs\times\sqrt{\frac{\F}{\kC}} \\
   &= \q \approx \result{\qP{-9}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc The force goes with $1/r^2$: half the distance, four times the force.
\begin{align}
 F' &= \FbF = 4\times\FO = \result{\FbP{-3}{2}}
\end{align}
Exactly this is what Coulomb found: the force falls off with the square of the distance.
\end{abcliste}
