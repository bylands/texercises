In 1785, Charles-Augustin de Coulomb measured the force between two small charged metal spheres with a torsion balance. Suppose the two spheres carry equal charges; their centres are \rsO\ apart (much more than their size of a few millimetres, so they act like point charges), and the balance shows a force of \FO.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc What is the charge of each sphere?
\abc What force would the balance show at half the distance?
\end{abcliste}

Take $k = \kCO$.
