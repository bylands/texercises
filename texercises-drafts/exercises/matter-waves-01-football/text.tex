A football (\mbO) flies at \vO\ into the goal, which is \wO\ wide. Light through a gap that wide would hardly be diffracted; waves are bent noticeably only by openings not much wider than their wavelength.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Calculate the de Broglie wavelength of the ball.

\abc How many wavelengths fit into the width of the goal?

\abc How slowly would the ball have to roll for its wavelength to equal the width of the goal?
\end{abcliste}
