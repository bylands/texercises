In a crystal of table salt (NaCl), positive \ce{Na+} and negative \ce{Cl-} ions alternate, \dnO\ apart. Each carries one elementary charge.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc How large is the force between two neighbouring ions?
\abc Consider a \ce{Na+} ion at the end of a row \ce{Na+} -- \ce{Cl-} -- \ce{Na+} and only these two neighbours. How large is the net force on it, and where does it point?
\end{abcliste}

Take $k = \kCO$ and $e = \qeO$.
