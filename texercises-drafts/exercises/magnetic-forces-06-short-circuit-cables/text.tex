Two straight power cables run parallel, \dO\ apart; one carries the current out, the other one back. In a short circuit, a current of \IO\ flows for a moment instead of the usual \IoO.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Do the cables attract or repel each other? Explain.

\abc Calculate the force on each metre of cable during the short circuit.

\abc By what factor is this force larger than with the usual current?
\end{abcliste}
