A light microscope cannot show details much smaller than the wavelength of light, about \lliO. A scanning electron microscope uses electrons accelerated through a voltage of $U = \UO$ instead. (Relativity changes the wavelength here by at most \SI{1.5}{\percent}; neglect it.)

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Calculate the wavelength of the electrons.

\abc How many times shorter than \lliO\ is it?

\abc By what factor must the voltage be changed to make the wavelength half as long?
\end{abcliste}
