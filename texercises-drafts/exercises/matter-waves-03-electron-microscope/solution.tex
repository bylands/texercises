\abc With $p = \sqrt{2 m_e e U}$:
\begin{align}
 \lambda &= \laF \\
   &= \frac{\nch}{\sqrt{2\times\ncme\times\nce\times\U}} \\
   &= \la \approx \result{\laP{-12}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc
\begin{align}
 \frac{\lambda_\text{light}}{\lambda} &= \frac{\lli}{\la} = \rat \approx \result{\ratP{0}{2}}
\end{align}
In practice, the lenses for electrons are far from perfect: a scanning electron microscope resolves about \SI{1}{\nano\m}, still hundreds of times better than light.

\abc Since $\lambda\propto 1/\sqrt{U}$, $\result{\text{four times the voltage}}$ halves the wavelength.
