UV-B light (\laO) from the Sun gives you a sunburn within an hour, while hours in front of a strong infrared heater (\lbO) do not. To change a molecule in a skin cell (to break or rearrange a bond in its DNA), about \EcO\ are needed.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Calculate the energy of a UV-B photon in electronvolts.

\abc Calculate the energy of an infrared photon of the heater in electronvolts.

\abc Explain why the heater causes no sunburn.
\end{abcliste}
