An old string of fairy lights consists of $n = \NO$ identical small bulbs in series, plugged into the \VO\ mains. The current is \IO.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc What is the voltage across each bulb, and what is the resistance of one bulb?
\abc When a bulb burns out, a tiny wire inside it shorts it out, so that the others keep shining. What current flows then? (Take the resistance of the other bulbs to stay the same.)
\end{abcliste}
