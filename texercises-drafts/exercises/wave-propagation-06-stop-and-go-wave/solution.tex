\begin{align}
 u &= \ujF = \frac{\xa-\xb}{\dt} = \uj \approx \result{\ukmhP{0}{2}}

$\result{\text{backwards, against the traffic}}$.

\abc The car crawls forwards at $v = \vcO$ while the jam moves backwards at $u$: they move towards each other, and their speeds add:
\begin{align}
 t &= \tjF \\
   &= \frac{\Lj}{\vc+\uj} \\
   &= \tj \approx \result{\tjmP{0}{2}}
\end{align}
(If the jam stood still, it would be \SI{9.0}{\minute}.) The cars (the medium) move forwards, the jam (the wave) backwards: a wave is not carried along by its medium.
