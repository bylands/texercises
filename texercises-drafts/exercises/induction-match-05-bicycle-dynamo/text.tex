A bottle dynamo is pressed against the tyre of a bicycle; it lights the lamp. In the dynamo, a magnet turns inside a coil.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc You ride twice as fast as before. How do the size and the frequency of the voltage of the dynamo change?

\abc At a red light you stand still. Is the lamp on or off? Explain.

\abc Is pedalling harder, just as easy or easier with the lamp switched on? Explain.
\end{abcliste}
