\begin{abcliste}
\abc At low frequencies the membranes (the capacitor) block: the current only flows outside the cells, and $Z = R_e$. The curve starts at $R_e \approx \result{\RexO}$.

\abc At high frequencies the capacitor acts like a wire: $R_e$ and $R_i$ are in parallel. The curve levels off at $Z \approx \ZhO$, and
\begin{align}
 \frac{1}{Z} = \frac{1}{R_e} + \frac{1}{R_i} \quad\Rightarrow\quad R_i &= \RinF \\
   &= \frac{\Rex\times\Zh}{\Rex - \Zh} \\
   &= \result{\RinP{0}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc At \SI{50}{\kilo\hertz}, $Z \approx \result{\ZfO}$: part of the current already passes through the cells.

Fat holds little water: the more fat, the fewer conducting paths and the larger the impedance. With height, weight and age, the scale estimates the body water, and from it the fat.
\end{abcliste}
