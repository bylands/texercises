\begin{abcliste}
\abc With $r$ the distance, the nucleus repels the alpha particle with
\begin{align}
 F &= \FF \\
   &= \kC\times\frac{158\times\left(\qe\right)^2}{\left(\ra\right)^2} \\
   &= \F \approx \result{\FP{0}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc With $m$ the mass of the alpha particle, Newton's second law gives
\begin{align}
 a &= \accF \\
   &= \frac{\F}{\ma} \\
   &= \acc \approx \result{\accP{0}{2}}
\end{align}
Rutherford saw some alpha particles bounce back: only a tiny, heavy, positive nucleus can push that hard.
\end{abcliste}
