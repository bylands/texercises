Davisson and Germer shot electrons accelerated through a voltage of $U = \UO$ onto a nickel crystal and counted the electrons scattered back at the angle $\varphi$. The atoms on the surface stand in rows $d = \dO$ apart. Like a reflection grating, they send many electrons in the direction where $d\cdot\sin\varphi = \lambda$.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Calculate the wavelength of the electrons.

\abc Calculate the angle of the strongest scattering.

\abc The voltage is raised. In which direction does the maximum move? Explain.
\end{abcliste}
