The solar wind carries electrons and protons to the Earth. Some of them are caught in the Earth's magnetic field, and near the poles they make the air glow: the aurora.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Describe the path of a particle caught in the Earth's magnetic field. Explain.

\abc Why does the aurora appear mostly near the poles?

\abc Many of the caught particles never reach the ground near the poles. Explain why.
\end{abcliste}
