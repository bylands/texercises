\begin{abcliste}
\abc For high frequencies the reactance $1/(2\pi f C)$ of the capacitor becomes small, and the capacitor acts like a wire: the curve levels off at $R \approx \result{\RO}$.

\abc Resistance and reactance add like the sides of a right triangle, $Z = \sqrt{R^2 + X_C^2}$. Where the reactance equals $R$,
\begin{align}
 Z &= \ZcF = \Zc \approx \ZcP{0}{2}
\end{align}
The curve reaches this value at $f_c \approx \result{\fcO}$.

\abc At the crossover the reactance equals $R$:
\begin{align}
 \frac{1}{2\pi f_c C} = R \quad\Rightarrow\quad C &= \CxF \\
   &= \frac{1}{2\pi\times\fx\times\R} \\
   &= \Cx \approx \result{\CxP{-6}{2}}
\end{align}
\end{abcliste}
