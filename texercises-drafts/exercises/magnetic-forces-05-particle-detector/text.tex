In a particle detector at CERN, a magnetic field of \BO\ runs along the beam pipe. A singly charged particle leaves a track that is bent on a circle with a radius of \rO.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Calculate the momentum of the particle.

\abc Another track is nearly straight. What does this tell about the particle?

\abc Two tracks bend in opposite directions. What does this tell about the two particles?
\end{abcliste}
