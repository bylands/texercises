\begin{abcliste}
\abc The alternating current in the coil makes a magnetic field that changes all the time. The changing flux through the base of the pan $\result{\text{induces currents in it}}$ (eddy currents), and these heat the pan from inside. (Heat does not flow from a hot coil, and the coil sends out no microwaves.)

\abc A direct current would make a constant field: the flux would not change, no currents would be induced, and the pan would $\result{\text{stay cold}}$.

\abc Glass does not conduct: no currents can flow in it, so a glass pan $\result{\text{does not heat}}$. (For the same reason the glass top stays cool too, apart from the heat of the pan.)

\abc The current reverses twice in each period:
\begin{align}
 n &= \nrF = 2\times\f = \result{\nrP{0}{2}}
\end{align}
\end{abcliste}
