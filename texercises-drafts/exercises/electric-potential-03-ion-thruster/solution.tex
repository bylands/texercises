\begin{abcliste}
\abc The mass of an ion is $m = A\,u = \AmO\times\ncu = \m$. Each ion (charge $e$) gains the energy $e U$, $\frac{1}{2} m v^2 = e U$:
\begin{align}
 v &= \velF \\
   &= \sqrt{\frac{2\times\nce\times\U}{\m}} \\
   &= \vel \approx \result{\velP{3}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc The current $I$ is the charge per second; each ion carries $e$, so $I/e$ ions leave per second, a mass flow of
\begin{align}
 \frac{\Delta m}{\Delta t} &= \mdotF = \frac{\I\times\m}{\nce} = \mdot
\end{align}
Every second this mass leaves with the speed $v$: the momentum carried away per second is the thrust,
\begin{align}
 F &= \frac{\Delta m}{\Delta t}\,v = \FF \\
   &= \mdot\times\vel \\
   &= \F \approx \result{\FP{-3}{2}}
\end{align}
A tiny thrust, but it acts for years.
\end{abcliste}
