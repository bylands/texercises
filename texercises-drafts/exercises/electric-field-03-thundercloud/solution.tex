\begin{abcliste}
\abc Treat cloud and ground like the plates of a capacitor; the field is roughly uniform:
\begin{align}
 E &= \EF = \frac{\U}{\hc} = \result{\EP{3}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc This is far below the \EbO\ at which air breaks down (about \ratioP{0}{2} times less). But at sharp points (a tree, a mast, the tip of a growing leader) \emph{the field is many times stronger}: there the discharge starts.
\end{abcliste}
