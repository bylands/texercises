The needle of a moving-coil meter reaches the end of its scale at a current of \IcO; the coil has a resistance of \RmO.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc What voltage is across the meter when the needle is at the end of the scale?
\abc To measure currents up to \IaO, a resistor is connected in parallel with the meter, so that most of the current bypasses it. What resistance must it have?
\abc To use the meter as a voltmeter for up to \VmaxO, a resistor is connected in series with it instead. What resistance must this one have?
