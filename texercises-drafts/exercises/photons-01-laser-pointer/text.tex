A green laser pointer (\laO, \PlO) shines into someone's eye by mistake. The blink reflex closes the eye after about \tO. (Never point a laser at people: even a few milliwatts focused on the retina can harm it.)

\begin{abcliste}
\abc How many photons does the pointer emit per second?

\abc How many photons enter the eye before it closes?

\abc Does a red pointer (\lbO) of the same power emit more, fewer or the same number of photons per second? Explain.
\end{abcliste}
