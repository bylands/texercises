In a cyclotron, protons circle in a magnetic field of \BO\ between two hollow half discs (the ``dees''). An alternating voltage between the dees speeds the protons up twice per turn, so they spiral outwards. The dees have a radius of \RO.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Calculate the frequency of the alternating voltage (one cycle per turn).

\abc Calculate the speed and the kinetic energy (in \si{\mega\electronvolt}) of the protons when they leave the cyclotron at the edge of the dees.

\abc How would the final energy change with dees twice as large (and the same field)?
\end{abcliste}
