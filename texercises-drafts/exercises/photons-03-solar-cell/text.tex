In a silicon solar cell, a photon can lift one electron across the band gap of $E_G = \EgO$, so that it can drive a current. A photon with less energy does nothing; of a photon with more energy, only \EgO\ are used and the rest turns into heat.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Calculate the longest wavelength the cell can use.

\abc Infrared light of \lirO\ falls on the cell. Does it produce a current? Explain.

\abc What part of the energy of a photon of \laO\ turns into heat?
\end{abcliste}
