\abc Head-on, the alpha particle stops where its whole kinetic energy has turned into electric potential energy (the heavy nucleus hardly moves):
\begin{align}
 E_\mathrm{kin} = k\,\frac{2e\cdot Z e}{r_\mathrm{min}} \quad\Rightarrow\quad r_\mathrm{min} &= \rminF \\
   &= \nckc\times\frac{2\times\ZO\times\left(\nce\right)^2}{\Ek} \\
   &= \rmin \approx \result{\rminP{-15}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc The alpha particle comes this close and is still repelled as by a point charge: the positive charge of the gold atom, which is several thousand times larger, \emph{sits in a tiny nucleus}, smaller than this distance.
