The search coil of a metal detector ($L_0 = \LzO$ without metal) forms a parallel resonant circuit with a capacitor ($C = \CO$). Eddy currents in a coin under the coil weaken its magnetic field: the inductance drops a little, and the resonance moves. Measuring the impedance of the circuit, one finds its peak at $f_0 = \fzO$ without the coin and at $f_1 = \faO$ with the coin.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Show that $f_0$ is the resonance frequency of $L_0$ and $C$.
\abc What is the inductance $L_1$ with the coin?
\abc By how much did the coin lower the inductance?
\end{abcliste}
