Neighbouring antinodes of a standing wave are half a wavelength apart, so
\begin{align}
 \lambda &= \laF = 2\times\d = \la \approx \result{\laP{-2}{2}}
\end{align}
The speed of the waves is
\begin{align}
 c &= \lambda\cdot f = \vwF \\
   &= 2\times\d\times\f \\
   &= \vw \approx \result{\vwP{0}{2}}
\end{align}
This is the speed of light: microwaves are light of a much longer wavelength. (Measuring the spots to a millimetre gives $c$ to a few percent.)
