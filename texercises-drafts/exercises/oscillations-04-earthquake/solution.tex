From the recording: the amplitude (the largest displacement) is $A = \AO$, the period (from crest to crest) is $T = \TO$. The angular frequency is
\begin{align}
 \omega &= \omF = \frac{2\pi}{\T} = \om
\end{align}

\begin{abcliste}
\abc
\begin{align}
 \hat a &= \amaxF \\
   &= \A\times\left(\om\right)^2 \\
   &= \amax \approx \result{\amaxP{0}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc
\begin{align}
 \frac{\hat a}{g} &= \frac{\amax}{\ncg} = \rg \approx \result{\rgP{0}{2}}
\end{align}
More than \rdO\,$g$: a dangerous earthquake for buildings.
\end{abcliste}
