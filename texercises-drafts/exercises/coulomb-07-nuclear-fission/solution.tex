\begin{abcliste}
\abc Each nucleus acts like a point charge $Z\,e$ at its centre. With $Z_1 e$ and $Z_2 e$ the charges and $d$ the distance of the centres,
\begin{align}
 F &= \FF \\
   &= \kC\times\frac{\ZaO\times\ZbO\times\left(\qe\right)^2}{\left(\df\right)^2} \\
   &= \F \approx \result{\FP{3}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc The mass of the krypton nucleus is $m = A\,u$:
\begin{align}
 a &= \accF \\
   &= \frac{\F}{\AO\times\uu} \\
   &= \acc \approx \result{\accP{0}{2}}
\end{align}
This electric repulsion drives the fragments apart: most of the energy released in fission comes from it.
\end{abcliste}
