In an old television tube or an oscilloscope, an electron gun accelerates electrons from rest through \UO.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc What is the speed of the electrons?
\abc Is the classical formula for the kinetic energy good enough at this speed, or is relativity needed? Compare the kinetic energy with the rest energy $m_e c^2$ of the electron (\SI{511}{\kilo\electronvolt}).
\end{abcliste}
