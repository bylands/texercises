\abc The drum is charged negatively all over; where the laser shines, it becomes conducting and loses its charge. The negatively charged toner is repelled by the charged parts and settles only on the discharged ones, \emph{where the laser has removed the charge}: this is the image.

\abc The paper behind is charged \emph{positively}, and more strongly than the drum: its field pulls the negative toner off the drum onto the paper.
\end{abcliste}
