In a laser printer, a drum is charged negatively. A laser writes the page on it, and fine, negatively charged toner powder is brought close to the drum. Then paper rolls past the drum.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Where does the toner stick to the drum: where the laser has removed the charge, or where the drum is still negative?
\abc How must the paper be charged to pull the toner onto it?
\end{abcliste}
