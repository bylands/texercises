\begin{abcliste}
\abc Tilting the head turns the axes of the glasses against the polarization of the screen. By Malus's law, $I = I_0\cos^2\theta$, where $\theta$ is the angle between the polarization and the axis of the glasses:
\begin{align}
 \frac{I}{I_0} &= \shrF = \cos^2\left(\alO\right) = \shr \approx \result{\shrP{0}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc Solving Malus's law for the angle:
\begin{align}
 \theta &= \thtF = \arccos\sqrt{\srX} = \result{\thtP{0}{2}}
\end{align}
At \SI{90}{\degree} the screen looks black.
\end{abcliste}
