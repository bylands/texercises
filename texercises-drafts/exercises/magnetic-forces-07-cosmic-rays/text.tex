Cosmic rays are mostly fast protons from space. The Earth's magnetic field shields us from part of them. At the equator, the field points north, parallel to the ground.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Where is the shielding weakest: near the poles or near the equator? Explain.

\abc A proton comes straight down at the equator. In which direction is it deflected?
\end{abcliste}
