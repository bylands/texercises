A proton consists of two up quarks (charge $+\frac{2}{3}e$ each) and one down quark (charge $-\frac{1}{3}e$). In a toy model, they sit at the corners of an equilateral triangle with sides of \sqO.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc How large is the force of one up quark on the down quark?
\abc How large is the net force on the down quark?
\end{abcliste}

(In reality the strong force is much larger and holds the quarks together.) Take $k = \kCO$ and $e = \qeO$.
