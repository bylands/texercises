The simplest molecule, \ce{H2+}, has two protons \dhO\ apart and a single electron. Take the electron at the midpoint between them.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc What net force acts on each proton, and where does it point?
\abc Why is the net force on the electron zero?
\end{abcliste}

Take $k = \kCO$ and $e = \qeO$.
