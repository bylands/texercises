In a simple picture of a helium atom, two electrons circle the nucleus (charge $+\ZO e$) at a distance of \reO, always on opposite sides of it.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc How large is the attraction of the nucleus on one electron?
\abc What is the net force on this electron, taking into account the other electron, and where does it point?
\end{abcliste}

Take $k = \kCO$ and $e = \qeO$.
