A small bar magnet is dropped, north pole down, through a plastic tube with a short coil wound around it. A data logger records the voltage induced in the coil. (The voltage is counted as minus the rate of change of the flux, with the magnet's flux through the coil positive.)

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Sketch the induced voltage as a function of time, from before the magnet reaches the coil until after it has left it.

\abc Explain why the second pulse is higher than the first.

\abc Compare the areas between the two pulses and the time axis.
\end{abcliste}
