\begin{abcliste}
\abc Each proton carries the charge $e$. With $d$ the distance, the two positive charges repel each other with
\begin{align}
 F &= \FF \\
   &= \kC\times\frac{\left(\qe\right)^2}{\left(\dp\right)^2} \\
   &= \F \approx \result{\FP{0}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc The mass whose weight is that large:
\begin{align}
 m &= \mF \\
   &= \frac{\F}{\ncg} \\
   &= \m \approx \result{\mP{0}{2}}
\end{align}
A huge force on a tiny particle: it would push a proton away with an acceleration of about $10^{28}\,\si{\meter\per\second\squared}$.
\end{abcliste}
