In a helium nucleus, the centres of the two protons are about \dpO\ apart.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc How large is the electric force with which they repel each other?
\abc What mass would have a weight of this size?
\end{abcliste}

(That the nucleus still holds together is due to another force, the strong nuclear force.) Take $k = \kCO$ and $e = \qeO$.
