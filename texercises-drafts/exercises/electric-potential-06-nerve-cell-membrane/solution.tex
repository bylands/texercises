\begin{abcliste}
\abc Across the thin membrane the field is nearly uniform. With $U = \UO$ the voltage across it and $d$ its thickness,
\begin{align}
 E &= \EF = \frac{\U}{\dm} = \result{\EP{6}{2}}
\end{align}
This is more than the breakdown field of air (about \SI{3}{\mega\volt\per\meter}).

\abc The field points from high to low potential. The inside is negative: the field points \emph{inwards}.

\abc Going in, the \ce{Na+} ion (charge $e$) moves to a potential lower by \UO. A positive charge gains the energy
\begin{align}
 W &= \WF = e\times\UO = \result{\WP{-3}{2}}
\end{align}
\end{abcliste}
