\begin{abcliste}
\abc In parallel, every device is at the full battery voltage $V$. With $R_\mathrm{h}$, $R_\mathrm{t}$ and $R_\mathrm{w}$ the resistances of a headlamp, a tail lamp and the heater, the currents through the devices add up:
\begin{align}
 I &= \IF \\
   &= \VO\times\left(\frac{2}{\RhO}+\frac{2}{\RtO}+\frac{1}{\RwO}\right) \\
   &= \result{\IP{0}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc All of them together draw this current at the battery voltage:
\begin{align}
 R_\mathrm{tot} &= \RF \\
   &= \frac{\V}{\I} \\
   &= \R \approx \result{\RP{0}{2}}
\end{align}
This is smaller than the smallest single resistance, as always in a parallel circuit.
\end{abcliste}
