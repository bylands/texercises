\begin{abcliste}
\abc At the threshold the photon energy equals the work function, $\frac{hc}{\lambda_G} = W_A$:
\begin{align}
 \lambda_G &= \lgrF = \frac{\nch\times\ncc}{\WaO} = \lgr \approx \result{\lgrP{-6}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc Only photons with at least the work function release electrons, i.e.\ wavelengths up to $\lambda_G$. The photon energies are
\begin{align}
 E_1 &= \EaF = \Ea \approx \EaP{0}{3} \\
 E_2 &= \EbF = \Eb \approx \EbP{0}{3}
\end{align}
compared with $W_A = \WaO$: the device sees $\result{\text{only the \laO\ lamp}}$. Lamps of \lbO\ are used where an intruder's night-vision device should not see them.

\abc
\begin{align}
 \ssc{E}{kin,max} &= \EkF = \Ea - \Wa = \Ek \approx \result{\EkP{0}{2}}
\end{align}
just enough for the electrons to get out.
\end{abcliste}
