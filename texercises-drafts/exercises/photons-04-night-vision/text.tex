In a night-vision device, faint light falls on a photocathode with a work function of \WaO; the electrons it releases are multiplied and make a screen glow. Security cameras light the scene with infrared lamps of \laO\ or \lbO, invisible to the eye.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Calculate the longest wavelength the device can see.

\abc Which of the two infrared lamps does the device see?

\abc Calculate the largest kinetic energy of the electrons released by the light of \laO.
\end{abcliste}
