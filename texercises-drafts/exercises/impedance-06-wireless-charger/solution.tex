\begin{abcliste}
\abc In series, coil and capacitor cancel at the resonance: $Z$ has its minimum where $2\pi f_0 = 1/\sqrt{L C}$. Hence
\begin{align}
 C &= \CF \\
   &= \frac{1}{\left(2\pi\times\fz\right)^2\times\Lz} \\
   &= \C \approx \result{\CP{-9}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc The minimum of the solid curve lies at $f_1 \approx \result{\faO}$.

\abc The capacitor stays the same:
\begin{align}
 L_1 &= \LaF \\
   &= \frac{1}{\left(2\pi\times\fa\right)^2\times\C} \\
   &= \La \approx \result{\LaP{-6}{2}}
\end{align}
At the old frequency, the impedance is now about \ZoO\ instead of \ZzO: the current would drop to about a third. That is why a charger searches for the new minimum and adjusts its frequency when a phone is placed on it.
\end{abcliste}
