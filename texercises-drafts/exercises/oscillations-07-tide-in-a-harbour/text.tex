In a harbour, the water rises and falls harmonically by \dHO\ between low and high tide, with a period of \TO. It is high tide now. A boat can only leave while the water is at least \hbO\ above the low-tide level.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc
How much time is left for the boat to leave?

\abc
How fast (in metres per hour) does the water level change at most?
\end{abcliste}
