A drop-tower car falls freely and is then braked without contact: a copper fin under the car falls between strong magnets (\BmO). The car enters the brake at \vO; the fin is $h = \hfO$ wide where it passes between the magnets.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Calculate the voltage induced across the fin as it enters the field.

\abc Do the magnets brake the fin, pull it in faster, or not act on it at all while the fin moves into the field? Explain.

\abc And while the fin leaves the field?

\abc If the car stopped with the fin between the magnets, would the magnets hold it in place?
\end{abcliste}
