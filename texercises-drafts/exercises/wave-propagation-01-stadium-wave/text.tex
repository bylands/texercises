In a stadium, the spectators make a wave: each one stands up and sits down again, taking $\tau = \tuO$, just after the neighbour. The wave runs round the stadium at \vO; the stadium is $C = \CsO$ round.

\abc How long does the wave take for one lap?

\abc Sketch how the head of one spectator moves up and down as a function of time.
\end{abcliste}
