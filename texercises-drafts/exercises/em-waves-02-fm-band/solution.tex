The LC circuit oscillates at the frequency
\begin{align}
 f &= \frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{L C}} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad C = \frac{1}{4\pi^2 f^2 L}
\end{align}
A higher frequency needs a smaller capacitance. The top of the band needs
\begin{align}
 C_\text{min} &= \CminF \\
   &= \frac{1}{4\pi^2\times\left(\fb\right)^2\times\L} \\
   &= \Cmin \approx \result{\CminP{-12}{2}}
\end{align}
and the bottom of the band
\begin{align}
 C_\text{max} &= \CmaxF \\
   &= \frac{1}{4\pi^2\times\left(\fa\right)^2\times\L} \\
   &= \Cmax \approx \result{\CmaxP{-12}{2}}
\end{align}
The frequencies differ by a factor of $108/87.5 \approx 1.23$, the capacitances by its square, $\ratP{0}{3}$.
