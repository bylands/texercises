\begin{abcliste}
\abc The power is spread over a sphere:
\begin{align}
 I &= \IaF \\
   &= \frac{\Pe}{4\pi\times\left(\r\right)^2} \\
   &= \Ia \approx \result{\IaP{-3}{2}}
\end{align}
The intensity of an electromagnetic wave is $I = \frac{1}{2}c\,\varepsilon_0\hat E^2$, so the amplitude of the electric field is
\begin{align}
 \hat E &= \EaF \\
   &= \sqrt{\frac{2\times\Ia}{\ncc\times\nceps}} \\
   &= \Ea \approx \result{\EaP{0}{2}}
\end{align}

\abc $\EaP{0}{2} < \ElO$: the mast is $\result{\text{within the limit}}$. At twice the distance, the field would be half as strong.
\end{abcliste}
