A mobile phone mast beams its signal towards a house \rO\ away. In that direction it radiates as strongly as a source spreading \PeO\ evenly in all directions (its equivalent radiated power).

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Calculate the intensity and the amplitude of the electric field at the house.

\abc In Switzerland, the field of a mast at places where people stay for long must not exceed about \ElO\ (the installation limit; for simplicity compare it here with the amplitude). Is the mast within this limit?
\end{abcliste}
