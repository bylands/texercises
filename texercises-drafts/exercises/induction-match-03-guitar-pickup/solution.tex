\begin{abcliste}
\abc The magnetised steel string changes the flux through the coil as it vibrates: once up and down per vibration. The voltage has the frequency of the string, $\result{\fO}$.

\abc The voltage depends on how fast the flux changes, i.e.\ on how fast the string moves. It moves fastest as it $\result{\text{passes its middle position}}$. At its turning points (closest to or furthest from the magnets) it stops for a moment: no voltage.

\abc Nylon is not magnetised by the magnets of the pickup: a vibrating nylon string does not change the flux through the coil, so $\result{\text{no voltage}}$ is induced.
\end{abcliste}
