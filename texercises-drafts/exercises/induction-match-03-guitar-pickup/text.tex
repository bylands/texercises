The pickup of an electric guitar is a set of small magnets with a coil wound around them. The magnets magnetise the steel string above them. The string vibrates \fX\ times per second.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc What is the frequency of the voltage induced in the coil?

\abc When is the induced voltage largest in size: when the string passes its middle position, or when it is closest to or furthest from the magnets?

\abc What does the coil give with a nylon string instead of a steel string?
\end{abcliste}
