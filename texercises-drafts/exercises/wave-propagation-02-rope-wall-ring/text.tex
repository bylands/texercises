A rope, \LrO\ long, is tied to a hook in a wall. You flick the other end once upwards: a crest runs along the rope at \vO.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc After how long does the crest come back to your hand?

\abc How does it come back: upright, upside down, or not at all?

\abc The rope is now tied to a ring that can slide up and down a smooth pole instead. How does the crest come back now?
\end{abcliste}
