A gardener cuts into the cable of an electric hedge trimmer and touches the bare live wire, which is at \VO\ against the ground. The current flows through the body (resistance \RbO\ from hand to feet) and then through the shoes into the ground.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc With dry rubber-soled shoes, shoes and ground have a resistance of \RsO. What current flows through the body?
\abc Barefoot on wet grass, the resistance of the ground is only \RwO. What current flows through the body now?
\end{abcliste}

(Currents above about \SI{30}{\milli\ampere} can kill.)
