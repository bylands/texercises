In a research reactor, neutrons are slowed down to the kinetic energy $\ssc{E}{kin} = \EkO$. They fall on a rock-salt crystal whose lattice planes are $d = \dO$ apart. The neutrons are reflected strongly at the glancing angle $\theta$ for which Bragg's condition $2d\cdot\sin\theta = \lambda$ holds.

\begin{abcliste}
\abc Calculate the speed of the neutrons.

\abc Calculate their wavelength.

\abc Calculate the glancing angle of the first strong reflection.
\end{abcliste}
