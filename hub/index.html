Torque

Lever arms and torques: levers and tools, beams on supports, ladders, tipping over, and centres of mass.

Coulomb Force

Point charges: Coulomb’s law, factors, and net forces as vectors, from charges in a line to rankings and points of zero force.

Energy Conservation

Falls, throws, pendulums and springs: from energy in each state to speeds and heights, with numbers or formulas.

Force Concepts

Gravity, inertia, net force and interaction: spot the misconceptions behind tempting answers.

Force Systems

Boxes, ropes, pulleys and slopes: find forces and accelerations with Newton’s second law.

Electromagnetic Induction

From a flux graph to the voltage it induces and back, a loop moving through a field, Lenz's rule: the slope counts, not the value.

Motion Graphs

Speed and direction, value tables, stroboscope pictures, slopes and areas: from position to velocity and back.

Oscillations

Harmonic or not: equations of motion, their periods and graphs, and the speed and acceleration of an oscillating body.

Cyclic Processes

Processes of an ideal gas in p(V), p(T) and V(T) diagrams: describe, draw, read and switch between them.

Wave Propagation

Crests on a rope: how they travel, how a place moves, how they add up and how they are reflected at a fixed or free end.

Electric Field

Field lines and their rules, forces on charges, fields that add up, dipoles, and charges flying through a capacitor.

Electric Potential

Potential and voltage, equipotential lines, the potential of point charges, accelerated particles and the electronvolt.

Magnetic Forces

Hand rules for charges and currents, forces between currents, circles and helices of charged particles, and the velocity selector.

Photons

Photon energy and momentum, the photoelectric effect and the measurement of h, X-ray spectra and the Compton effect.

Matter Waves

The de Broglie wavelength and electron diffraction, single quanta at the double slit, the uncertainty relation and tunnelling.
